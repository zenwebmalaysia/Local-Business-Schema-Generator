# Local Business Schema Generator (Malaysia)

A free tool that creates LocalBusiness structured data (JSON-LD) for Malaysian businesses – so Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and other AI search tools can read your business name, type, address, phone, opening hours and social profiles without guessing.

**Use it online:** https://zenwebmalaysia.github.io/local-business-schema/

Built by [ZenWeb](https://zenweb.my/), a digital marketing agency in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia (SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, web design and development).

## Features

- 30 business types in plain language (clinic, dentist, café/kopitiam, tuition centre, law firm, renovation contractor, car workshop and more), mapped to the right schema.org type.
- All 16 Malaysian states and federal territories; country set to `MY`.
- Phone numbers converted to international format (`03-1234 5678` → `+60312345678`).
- Opening hours per day, grouped automatically into `OpeningHoursSpecification`.
- Optional map coordinates (paste "lat, lng" straight from Google Maps), Google Maps link, cuisine and menu for food businesses, and `sameAs` social profiles.
- Live checks for missing or invalid fields, including coordinates outside Malaysia.
- Copy the code or download it as a file.

## How to use

1. Fill in the form (it starts with a made-up example – click **Clear the example**).
2. Copy the code.
3. Add it to your homepage or contact page (WordPress: a header/footer code plugin or your SEO plugin's custom schema; Shopify: `theme.liquid`; Wix: Custom code).
4. Test the page with Google's [Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results).

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.
