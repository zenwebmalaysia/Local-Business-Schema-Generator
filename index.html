    Where to put the code

    • WordPress – use a header-and-footer code plugin (such as WPCode) and add the code to your homepage, or use the custom schema feature in Rank Math or Yoast. Do not paste it into a normal text block.
    • Wix – Settings → Custom code → add to the head of the homepage, or use the page’s SEO “Structured data markup”.
    • Shopify – Online Store → Themes → Edit code → theme.liquid, just before </head>.
    • Custom website – paste it inside <head> or before </body> on the homepage or contact page.

    Add it once. If your SEO plugin already outputs LocalBusiness or Organization schema, update that instead of adding a second copy.

    Keep your details the same everywhere

    Use the same business name, address and phone number (NAP) on your website, Google Business Profile, Facebook page and directory listings. Matching details are one of the strongest local SEO signals – and they stop AI tools from mixing you up with another business.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is LocalBusiness schema?

    It is a short block of code that states your business facts – name, type, address, phone, hours and profiles – in a format machines read directly. Visitors do not see it; search engines and AI tools do.

    Will it get me a better Google ranking?

    Schema is not a ranking boost by itself. It helps Google understand your business and show correct details, and it supports your Google Business Profile. Rankings still depend on content, reviews, links and relevance.

    Does it help with ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews?

    AI search tools build answers from pages they can understand. Clear, consistent facts on your own website make it easier for them to describe your business correctly. It is one step – together with good content and listings – towards being mentioned.

    Which business type should I choose?

    Pick the most specific type that fits, such as Dentist instead of MedicalClinic. If nothing matches, LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService is fine.

    My business has more than one branch. What now?

    Create one schema block per branch, each with its own address, phone and hours, and put it on that branch’s page.
